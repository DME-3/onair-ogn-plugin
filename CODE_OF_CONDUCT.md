# Community conduct

Treat participants respectfully. Give specific, constructive technical
feedback and welcome contributors with different levels of experience.
Harassment, discrimination, threats and publishing another person's private
information are unacceptable.

Keep discussion focused on the project. Do not post captured aircraft or
device identities, locations or other personal data without authorization.

Report conduct concerns to a repository maintainer using GitHub's available
private contact or reporting controls. Do not disclose sensitive evidence in
a public issue. Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, close disruptive
threads or restrict participation. Decisions should be proportionate and
consider the safety and privacy of affected people.
