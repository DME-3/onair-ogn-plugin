src/ogn1_checks.inc contains the numeric parity-check constants identifying
the public OGN1 air-interface code, transcribed from glidernet/ogn-tracker's
ldpc.h at the revision above. The LDPC encoder/decoder, TEA implementation
and explicit field parser are independently written here; no tracker
implementation source is bundled. The reference codeword used in the tests
is the public example published on the OGNTP wiki, not a captured packet.

No recordings, private decoded reports, identifying capture content or
private test artifacts are included in this source or installable package.
